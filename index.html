Why “39 Wishes”

Where Highway 39 meets the river, and your wishes come true

The property sits right off Texas Highway 39 along the Guadalupe. This land has been in our family for three generations. My great uncle BJ Sublett and his wife Effie built the main house in 1931, and the room that is now the Guest Kitchenette belonged to their daughter Audrey. We still call it Audrey’s Room.

We have remodeled both buildings with guests in mind, and it has been our dream to share this corner of the Hill Country with others. We live on the property and are happy to help, or to give you all the quiet you came for. You take the lead.

Stay 01 · Whole house

The River House

A stone-and-wood Hill Country home with a covered porch, an open deck over the water and space for up to eight. Bring the family, fire up the grill and let the kids fish off the dock.

Inside, you’ll find a fully equipped kitchen, a living room with a stone fireplace, three bedrooms and two bathrooms. Most of the lights can be controlled with Alexa.

Two bedrooms each have a king bed, and the attic sleeps four on twin XL beds with its own living area — a space the kids will claim as their own.

Outside is where the days go: coffee on the covered porch, lunch on the deck, a paddle upriver in the afternoon and the firepit once the sun drops behind the cypress trees.

Stay 02 · Private room for two

Guest Kitchenette

Audrey’s old room, rebuilt as a bright, private suite for two. It sits completely separate from the main house, with its own patio looking out over the river.

The room has a comfortable king sized bed, a kitchenette with a Keurig and small fridge, a walk-in tiled shower and a mini-split air conditioner that keeps things cool through a Texas summer. Tell Alexa to turn the lights off when you head out to the patio.

It’s a good fit for couples, solo travelers and anyone passing through Kerrville who wants the river without the bustle.

On the property

Life on the Guadalupe

The river is right out the back door. Everything below is included with your stay.

Kayaks & paddle board

Grab a kayak or the stand-up paddle board. Life jackets are in the bin near the fishing poles.

Swimming

About 10 feet deep off the dock. Warm water, with cold spots from the springs that feed the river.

Fishing

Perch and catfish near the dock. Rods, tackle, buckets and camp chairs available. Ages 17+ need a Texas fishing license.

Firepit evenings

Adirondack chairs, string lights and a stone firepit by the water. Stay out for the sunset and the stars.
